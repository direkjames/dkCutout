How to use WalangBackground

1. Add your photo

Drag a photo onto the box, click it to choose a file, or paste one with Ctrl+V. PNG, JPG and WEBP work.

Upscale: make the picture 2× or 4× bigger. Best and Fast use AI to rebuild real detail (downloaded once), Classic is a plain smooth resize. Huge photos are processed in small pieces so your browser stays happy. If a size is greyed out, it is too big for your device.

Quality: Best gives cleaner results. Fast is quicker on older phones and PCs. The AI downloads once the first time you use it (40–80 MB), then it's saved in your browser.

2. Check the result

Compare lets you drag the slider left and right to see the photo before and after. The checkered pattern means transparent.

3. Fix anything the AI missed

  • Subject solidity: parts of the person look faded or see-through? Slide it up. Edges look too hard? Slide it down.
  • Fill small holes: automatically patches small gaps inside the person. Leave it on unless it fills something that should be see-through.
  • Restore brush: paint back parts the AI removed by mistake. The original photo shows faintly so you can see what to paint.
  • Erase brush: paint away leftover background.
  • Size changes the brush size, Undo removes your last brush stroke, and Reset removes all brush strokes.

4. Download

Download PNG saves your cut-out at the same size as the original photo, with a transparent background. New image starts over.

🔒 Your photos never leave your device. Everything happens in your browser.
